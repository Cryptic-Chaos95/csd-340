01 / Sports & community

Beyond the ballpark

Sierra's love of sports is about more than the game. While completing her degree, she worked as a Community and Fan Engagement Manager for the Montgomery Biscuits. She photographed this baseball game during her time with the team, capturing a memorable sunset above the field.

She also follows football, Formula 1, the WNBA, and professional wrestling, drawn to athletes’ personal stories and strong personalities.

02 / Art, history & culture

Finding meaning in places

An art history course sparked Sierra's interest in museums and visual culture. This photograph of the Smithsonian Institution Building in Washington, D.C., reflects her love of exploring places where art and history intersect.

She is also interested in historical fiction and true crime, particularly the psychology and sociology behind people's choices.
