Meet Sierra

For Sierra, life is an opportunity to learn, create, and experience the world from different perspectives. She describes herself as outgoing, understanding, and someone who loves cultural experiences. Her personality combines creativity, confidence, and curiosity, shaping both her interests and her professional ambitions.

Growing up in a close-knit family gave Sierra a strong foundation. Surrounded by family members who valued communication and were skilled public speakers, she developed an appreciation for the power of words and self-expression. This influence led her to study communications with a focus in public relations at Auburn University at Montgomery (AUM). One of her favorite courses was Ethics and Law, and earning her degree represented the beginning of a lifetime of learning.

While completing her degree, Sierra worked as a Community and Fan Engagement Manager for the Montgomery Biscuits. The role connected her love of sports with her interest in communication and community relationships. After graduating, she worked as a Communications Specialist with the Alabama Securities Commission. She currently works as a Charter School Program (CSP) Grant Community and Communications Specialist, continuing to develop her career through public-facing communication and engagement.

Creativity has always been important to Sierra. Watching dancers on MTV as a child sparked her interest in dance and gymnastics, which offered both artistic expression and discipline. She also participated in college cheerleading. Overcoming major injuries taught her patience and helped her grow.

Her curiosity reaches far beyond performance. An art history class deepened her appreciation for art and museums, and she enjoys historical fiction, the psychology and sociology of true crime, and cultural experiences. One of her favorite travel memories is visiting Montana for a wedding, seeing Glacier National Park, and going up a mountain for the first time.

Sports and entertainment are another major part of her life. She enjoys football, the WNBA, Formula 1, and professional wrestling, especially the stories and personalities of athletes. She admires confident figures such as Lewis Hamilton, Rhea Ripley, Paige Bueckers, and Jon Moxley.

Sierra counts earning her degree, having her own apartment, and working in her chosen field immediately after graduation among her proudest milestones. Looking ahead, she hopes to earn Accreditation in Public Relations (APR) and a master’s degree. Through it all, she wants people to know that she values the creative process and does not take life too seriously.
